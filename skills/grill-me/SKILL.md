# Grill Me

Run a `grilling` session on the plan, design, or idea supplied by the user.

Return the decision handoff produced by `grilling`, then stop. Do not implement
the plan or invoke another skill.

Adapted from Matt Pocock's
[grill-me](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/productivity/grill-me)
skill.
